Special Functions (not on external pins):
------------------------------------------
GP23:  Power save control (switches between VBUS and VSYS)
GP24:  VBUS sense (detects USB power)
GP25:  On-board LED
GP29:  ADC3 (VSYS/3 voltage monitor)
ADC4:  Temperature sensor (internal)

Quick Reference by Function:
=============================

SPI0:
  RX:   GP0, GP4, GP16, GP20
  CSn:  GP1, GP5, GP17, GP21
  SCK:  GP2, GP6, GP18, GP22
  TX:   GP3, GP7, GP19

SPI1:
  RX:   GP8, GP12, GP28
  CSn:  GP9, GP13
  SCK:  GP10, GP14, GP26
  TX:   GP11, GP15, GP27

I2C0:
  SDA:  GP0, GP4, GP8, GP12, GP16, GP20, GP28
  SCL:  GP1, GP5, GP9, GP13, GP17, GP21

I2C1:
  SDA:  GP2, GP6, GP10, GP14, GP18, GP22, GP26
  SCL:  GP3, GP7, GP11, GP15, GP19, GP27

UART0:
  TX:   GP0, GP12, GP16, GP28
  RX:   GP1, GP13, GP17
  CTS:  GP2, GP14, GP18
  RTS:  GP3, GP15, GP19

UART1:
  TX:   GP4, GP8, GP20
  RX:   GP5, GP9, GP21
  CTS:  GP6, GP10, GP22, GP26
  RTS:  GP7, GP11, GP27

ADC Inputs:
  ADC0: GP26 (Pin 31)
  ADC1: GP27 (Pin 32)
  ADC2: GP28 (Pin 34)
  ADC3: GP29 (not on external pin - VSYS/3 monitor)
  ADC4: Temperature sensor (internal)

PWM Channels:
  PWM0: A=GP0,GP16  B=GP1,GP17
  PWM1: A=GP2,GP18  B=GP3,GP19
  PWM2: A=GP4,GP20  B=GP5,GP21
  PWM3: A=GP6,GP22  B=GP7
  PWM4: A=GP8       B=GP9
  PWM5: A=GP10,GP26 B=GP11,GP27
  PWM6: A=GP12,GP28 B=GP13
  PWM7: A=GP14      B=GP15
